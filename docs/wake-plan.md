# Persistent wakes

`cardd` polls every 15 seconds. Thresholds share `CARDD_TIMING_SCALE` for isolated end-to-end tests. No reminder queries GitHub.

| Obligation                                                                                    | First reminder                                         | Repeats                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Worker never connected or disconnected                                                        | Mate after 2 minutes                                   | Every 5 minutes; once for an incident whose retries and replacements are spent, until a new incident or process generation                                                                                                   |
| Worker has no output or tool activity                                                         | Mate after 5 minutes                                   | Every 5 minutes                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| One running turn has no report, commit, or file change                                        | Mate after 1 hour                                      | Every 5 minutes                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Worker idle without a handoff                                                                 | Worker after 2 minutes; mate 5 minutes after the nudge | Mate every 5 minutes                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Runtime failure or failed compaction                                                          | Existing incident notice                               | Mate every 5 minutes until recovery                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Runtime failure of a second mate's worker with no recovery left                               | Main once, 5 minutes after the incident opened         | Never                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Finished open card                                                                            | Owning mate after 10 minutes                           | Every 10 minutes                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Cards waiting on a mate: with-mate, merging, holds on it, and for main every captain decision | Each mate, one bundled list, after 10 minutes          | Every 10 minutes; none when empty; a dated wait is a line in the list, as information when it is watched, an outside date, or the captain's later, and for action when it is an idle park; an expired hold is announced once |
| Only watched dated waits, outside dates, or the captain's later                               | Each mate, one information list, after 24 hours        | Every 24 hours while such a wait lasts; a list that was due for them counts                                                                                                                                                  |
| [Idle open card](neglect.md#who-is-told-and-when)                                             | Main, in its bundled list, after 4 hours               | Every 10 minutes while it is idle; the owning mate keeps its own reminders                                                                                                                                                   |
| Message not taken in                                                                          | Resend after 2 minutes while the receiver is up        | Every 2 minutes; main after 3 resends, then every 15 minutes                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Reply owed                                                                                    | Resend after 2 minutes                                 | Every 5 minutes until answered or withdrawn                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Lost long job                                                                                 | Lost-job notice: legacy after 3 missed heartbeats, registered on confirmed wrapper exit | Mate every 5 minutes until rerun or dropped                                                                                                                                                                                  |

Output and tool updates refresh activity. After tools finish, Git HEAD and the timestamps and sizes of changed or untracked files refresh productive progress when they actually change. Shell edits and commits with Git options count too. A running tool that emits output is not stalled. Output alone does not prevent spinning.

Each reminder uses the obligation, occurrence, and due tier as its identity. A down mate receives no new reminders. On return it receives the current tier, not a backlog of missed tiers. Closing or completion ends the obligation. The bundled list and the finished-card reminder are for the owning mate. A captain decision in main's list is not a prompt to write to the captain: main lists captain decisions to him again only when he has written since the last list. A dated hold is watched when it names a job, a schedule, a worker, or a watcher. A watched hold, an outside date, and the captain's later stop the card's other reminders until the date, and the bundle lists them as information. A mate whose only open items are such holds hears one information list a day. A declared watcher, an outside date, and the captain's later stay information lines until their date, however old. A dated hold with nothing running (an idle park) stays on the bundle for action, and the card's other reminders continue. A wait on the mate's action is `blocked` and stays on the list. Progress reports do not resolve unanswered replies. Scheduled wakes continue while replies remain open.

Runtime failure recovery still uses `recover`. Repeating a notice never retries a model or restarts a process automatically. No programmed escalation substitutes for a healthy second mate deciding when to ask main, except for a runtime failure that has no recovery left: main is told of it once. [Runtime incidents](code.md#runtime-incidents) says when no recovery is left.

For merge verification outside the timed check, see [Closing](code.md#closing).

`docs/spec.md` needs its timed-check and escalation descriptions updated by its maintainer to match this plan.

## Worker checkpoints

A worker's dated pause keeps a Working or In review card off the bundle until its checkpoint. It does not hide an open hold or a Merging card. A pause with no job running reaches the mate as a routine report. When the checkpoint passes with no [running job that covers the card](neglect.md#what-counts-as-a-real-change) and no worker entry after the checkpoint, the card is on the owning mate's ten-minute bundle for action. It leaves when the worker reports or the mate steers. See `e2e/cardd/pause-watch.test.ts` for the repeated-job case.
